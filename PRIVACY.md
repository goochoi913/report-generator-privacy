Report Generator – Privacy Policy
Last updated: 09/27/2025

What the extension does
Report Generator creates a student progress report from the AppSheet page you have open. When you click “Generate Report,” the extension reads the visible table rows/columns for your selected date range, sends that text to Google’s Gemini API to generate an “Overall Progress Summary,” and assembles a local HTML report in your browser.

Data we handle
• Website content from the current AppSheet tab (e.g., dates, notes, hours) that you choose to include.

How we use your data
• Solely to generate your report and the AI summary.
• No analytics, advertising, or profiling.

Sharing
• The selected text is sent to Google’s Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) only to generate the summary. We do not sell data.

Storage & retention
• We do not store your data on our servers. Processing happens in memory. The report is created locally as a blob: URL.

Security
• All network requests to the Gemini API use HTTPS.

Your choices
• Do not click “Generate Report” if you do not want the page content sent to the Gemini API.
• You can uninstall the extension at any time.
